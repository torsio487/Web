Paradox

Time Manipulating Master Thief

Overview

Paradox excels at tactical isolation and controlling engagement terms. Using time-warping technology, Paradox can stop time, swap positions with enemies, and lock down key targets.

Abilities

1. Pulse Grenade

Throws a grenade that repeatedly pulses, damaging and slowing enemies in its radius.

2. Time Wall

Creates a temporal barrier that stops enemy projectiles and severely slows enemies who walk through it.

3. Kinetic Carbine

Charges up a powerful shot that gains bonus damage and movespeed while aiming.

4. Paradox Swap (Ultimate)

Fires a projectile that swaps the positions of Paradox and the target enemy hero on hit.

Lore & Background

A notorious master thief whose identity remains shrouded in rumor, Paradox utilizes illegal temporal artifacts to pull off impossible heists. In the arena, her spatial-temporal control manipulates the battlefield in unpredictable ways.
