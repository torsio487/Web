Hero Focus: Paradox

Biography & Lore

Paradox is both the name of this Hero and the name of their Thieves' Guild, specializing in stealing the untouchable and breaking into ultra-secure facilities. Their motives are unclear, except for a certain level of delight that its members seem to take in snubbing the world's biggest corporations and governments.

Signature Abilities

  • Pulse Grenade: Throws time grenade that explodes several times and getting bigger.
  • Time Wall: Places time wall that slows down enemies and projectiles.
  • Kinetic Carbine: Fires empowered charged shot that freezes foes in time.
  • Paradoxical Swap: Switches physical locations with a targeted enemy hero.
